# Understanding the Weighted Mean

## 中文版笔记

## 学习目标

- 理解算术平均与加权平均的区别。
- 检查权重之和，并解释计算结果。

## 关键概念

算术平均为每个数值分配相同权重。加权平均允许不同数值具有不同的重要程度。

> 公开示例：数值仅用于演示笔记中的 KaTeX 公式与表格。

## 主要内容

### 从相同权重到不同权重

两个数值 $80$ 和 $90$ 的算术平均为：

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{80 + 90}{2} = 85.
$$

若对应的权重分别为 $0.4$ 和 $0.6$，则加权平均为：

$$
\bar{x}_w = 0.4 \times 80 + 0.6 \times 90 = 86.
$$

| 数值 | 权重 | 对结果的贡献 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 80 | 0.4 | 32 |
| 90 | 0.6 | 54 |
| 合计 | 1.0 | 86 |

### 检查一般形式

当权重尚未归一化时，应除以权重之和：

$$
\bar{x}_w = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i x_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i}, \qquad \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i \ne 0.
$$

对于非负且不全为零的权重，结果应位于输入数值的最小值与最大值之间。这里 $86$ 位于 $80$ 与 $90$ 之间，也更接近权重较大的 $90$。

## 小结

先明确每个权重代表什么，再检查是否需要归一化，最后解释结果。权重相同时，加权平均退化为算术平均。

## English Version

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the difference between the arithmetic mean and the weighted mean.
- Check the sum of weights and interpret the result.

## Key Concepts

The arithmetic mean gives every value equal weight. The weighted mean allows values to have different levels of importance.

> Public example: the numbers only demonstrate KaTeX formulas and tables in a note.

## Main Content

### From Equal to Unequal Weights

The arithmetic mean of $80$ and $90$ is:

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{80 + 90}{2} = 85.
$$

With corresponding weights $0.4$ and $0.6$, the weighted mean becomes:

$$
\bar{x}_w = 0.4 \times 80 + 0.6 \times 90 = 86.
$$

| Value | Weight | Contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 80 | 0.4 | 32 |
| 90 | 0.6 | 54 |
| Total | 1.0 | 86 |

### Check the General Form

If the weights have not been normalized, divide by their sum:

$$
\bar{x}_w = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i x_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i}, \qquad \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i \ne 0.
$$

For nonnegative weights that are not all zero, the result lies between the smallest and largest input values. Here, $86$ lies between $80$ and $90$ and is closer to the more heavily weighted $90$.

## Summary

Identify what each weight means, check whether normalization is needed, and interpret the result. Equal weights reduce the weighted mean to the arithmetic mean.
